Common questions

Simple answers, no surprises.

Do I need to register for the full mySales ERP?

No. This lightweight storefront works separately from the mySales ERP. You can add products directly using your private store manager.

Can I use it for free?

Yes. Storefront creation is free during this pilot. We will communicate any future pricing clearly before charging for an optional service.

Can I change my photos and prices later?

Yes, any time. Save your private management link when your store is created. Keep it confidential because it gives access to editing the store.

Do customers pay on the website?

Not in this pilot. Customers browse your catalogue, add products to an enquiry and send the details through WhatsApp. You confirm availability and payment directly.

What if I lose my private link?

Contact the mySales operator to help replace it. For security, the original private link cannot be recovered, and the old one is revoked when replaced.
